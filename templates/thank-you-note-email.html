Thank-You Note (Email)

Send within 24 hours — sooner if the next round is close. Short beats eloquent: four sentences, one real detail, out the door.

Pick a subject line (short does better)

Thank you — [your name], [role] interview, [day of week]
Great meeting you about the [role] role
Enjoyed our conversation — [your name]
My own: ______________________________________________

Copy, change the brackets, send

Hi [they usually say First Name],

Thank you for the conversation today about [role]. I especially enjoyed talking about [ONE REAL THING — a project, a problem they described, a joke that landed]. It made [my interest in the work / the team's way of doing things] more concrete for me.

[Optional, one sentence: a short reply to something they asked, or a link to one example of your work.]

Happy to provide anything else that helps your decision. Thanks again for your time.

Best,
[your name]
[phone]

Rules my future self will thank me for

  • One specific moment from their answers — proof I was listening, not a template.
  • No apologizing, no over-explaining, no new worries raised for the first time.
  • Read it once out loud. If it sounds like me, send. If it sounds like a robot, un-formal it.
  • One person, one note — every interviewer who talked with me gets their own (copy the structure, change the detail).
  • If there was no interviewer email, ask for it; that's a normal request, not a favor.
If you can't think of a "real thing" — pick the smallest true one: the way they described the schedule, the team, even the questions they asked. Small-but-true beats big-but-empty every single send.

Then close the draft, mark it sent in the reflection log, and go do the evening. The waiting has homework only if you give it some.
