STAR Story Worksheet

Most interview is built on "tell me about a time when..." questions. This sheet turns your real experiences into ready stories. Rough words are fine — nobody ever sees this page but you.

STAR means: Situation (what was happening) · Task (what you needed to do) · Action (what YOU actually did) · Result (what happened next — numbers if you have them).

First, pick your three stories

Try to have one story each of: working with people, a problem you solved, and something you're proud of. Skim the common prompts below; three of them usually match any interview.

  • a time you helped a customer or coworker who was frustrated
  • a mistake you caught (or made) and what you did next
  • a day that was too busy, and how you decided what came first
  • something you learned fast when you had to
  • a change you suggested that actually got tried
  • a time you helped someone on the team succeed (it still counts)
  • ____________________________________________________________
Write the answer out in messy handwriting first. Speaking it out loud later teaches you the shape of it — the words themselves will come on interview day.
