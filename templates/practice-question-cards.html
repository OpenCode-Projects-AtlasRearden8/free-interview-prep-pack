Practice Question Cards

Print, cut along the dashed lines, practice out loud. Score yourself kindly: rough is where practice starts, not where it fails.

Do one card at a time. Say the whole answer out loud — thinking it is a different skill than saying it. Then circle where you honestly landed. On a good day, rough-to-solid in three tries is normal. The playful-to-ready scale below is the one the whole pack uses: 1 = it came out rough (fine!) · 2 = got there eventually · 3 = solid · 4 = ready to say in the room
